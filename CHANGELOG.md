# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 0.1.0 - Unreleased

### Added

- Rest the cursor on an image or a link to one in your browser, and the full-size image appears beside it. Galleries and multi-photo posts step through with the scroll wheel.
- Videos from Reddit, X, Bluesky, Instagram, Streamable, and Imgur play in the preview, and YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, and Twitch links play in each site's own player.
- On YouTube, hovering a video title or channel name shows a card about its creator, with Subscribe once you sign in.
- In Finder, resting on a file or folder shows a card with its path and details, and which apps are using it.
- Images larger than the download limit you set show their size first, with Load Anyway.
- Previews stay in your browser unless you allow other apps, with options to skip email apps, stick to known sites, or wait for a key.
- A welcome window walks you through turning on Accessibility access.
- HoverEnhance checks for updates and installs them for you.
